# Stats

**Sidebar > Photos > Stats** shows how you shoot and what you keep. A
**keeper** is a photo you flagged **Pick** - stars don't count. Videos aren't
counted; archived photos are (you shot them).

- **At a glance:** cards for photos, days out shooting, keepers and your
  keeper rate, your most used camera, lens and focal length, and your busiest
  hour and day of the week. With no Picks yet, Keepers says "None yet" and the
  charts show counts only.
- **Breakdown by** lens, camera, focal length, aperture, ISO or shutter
  speed: a bar each - its length is how many photos, the coloured part how
  many you kept, with the figures in a column of their own. Handy for "do I keep more at f/2.8
  or f/4?" or "is ISO 6400 worth it on this camera?".
- **Focal lengths on** a lens: where you really use a zoom - in ranges
  (20-29 mm, 30-39 mm...) when a zoom has many focal lengths.
- **Photos per month**, with the number of shoot days.
- **Your year:** choose a year and **Make a recap picture** - a 1080 x 1350
  image of your year in numbers, saved to the Create folder to share.
