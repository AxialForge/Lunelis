# Places

Every photo with a location gets a place tag - **Places > Italy > Lazio >
Rome** - worked out on this PC from a list of about 32,000 towns and cities
built into Lunelis (from GeoNames, CC BY 4.0). Nothing is looked up online.
Place tags work like any tag: the Tag filter, search ("Rome"), smart albums
and XMP sidecars.

## Where the location comes from

- **The camera's GPS** (phones, and cameras with GPS or a phone link).
- **A pin you drop on the Map** - for photos without GPS, or to correct one.
  A pin wins over the camera's GPS. The photo files never change; the pin is
  kept in the catalog.

## Which tag a photo gets

| Where the photo was taken | Tag |
|---|---|
| Within 40 km of a town on the list | Places > Country > Region > Town |
| In a country, but far from any town (a trail, a beach) | Places > Country > Unknown |
| Far from anywhere on the list (at sea) | Places > Unknown |
| No location at all (only if you turn it on) | Places > No location |

Near a big city its suburbs give way to it: a photo in Brooklyn is tagged
Brooklyn, one in central Paris is tagged Paris (not its arrondissement), but a
small town keeps its own name.

## Putting photos on the map

1. Select the photos in the library (any number).
2. **Photo > Set location on the map...** (`Ctrl+Shift+L`). The Map opens
   with "Click where these photos were taken".
3. Zoom and drag to the spot and click. Lunelis shows the place it found
   ("Put these 12 photos at Rome, Lazio, Italy?") and asks first.

Or drag the photos from the library onto **Map** in the sidebar, hold a
moment until the Map opens, and drop them on the spot.

**Map > Without a location (N)** shows the photos that still have none, so
you can select them and place them. **Photo > Remove the pinned location**
takes a pin off (the camera's own GPS, if any, counts again).

## Settings

**Settings > Library > Places**:

- **Give photos a place tag after each scan** - on by default.
- **Tag photos without any location Places > No location** - off by default
  (on a library from cameras without GPS it would tag most photos). Handy
  while you work through the photos that still need a pin.
- **Name places now** - brings every place tag up to date at once.

A place tag you remove by hand isn't put back, unless the photo's location
changes.

## Strangers - People > Unknown

The same idea for faces: someone you don't know - in a crowd, a street, a
public place - is a **stranger**, and the photo gets **People > Unknown**.
See [People](People.md#strangers).
