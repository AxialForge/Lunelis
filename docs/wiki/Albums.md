# Albums

**Albums** (sidebar, under Photos) shows your collections as cover tiles in
three sections. Click any tile to see its photos in the library. A chip at
the top, like **Album: Trip**, shows what you're looking at; its **x** takes
you back to everything. The photo view, ratings and the timeline all work
there as usual.

## Your albums

**Drag photos onto an album:** select them in the library and drag them onto
**Albums** in the sidebar - hold them there a moment and the Albums page
opens - then drop them on an album.

Collections you make yourself. A photo can be in as many albums as you like.

- **New album:** the dashed tile. Or select photos in the library and choose
  **Photo > Album > New album from selection...**.
- **Add photos:** select them, then **Photo > Album > Add to album...**
  (`Ctrl+Shift+A`). Pick an album, or make a new one right there.
- **Take photos out:** open the album, select them, then **Photo > Album >
  Remove from this album**.
- **Rename or remove an album:** right-click its tile.

Removing an album, or a photo from an album, never touches the photo itself.
The photo view's Info panel lists the albums a photo is in.

## Events

Your [events](Events.md) (trips, shoots, days out), each with its dates and
count. A photo is in at most one event. **Event suggestions...** opens the
page that finds events in your folder names and capture times. Right-click
an event to rename or remove it.

## The Archive

Photos you want to keep but not see - old screenshots, near-misses, things
for later. Select them and choose **Photo > Archive** (`Ctrl+Shift+H`).

- They leave the Library grid, search, filters, events and the automatic
  albums. Their files don't move.
- They stay in any album you put them in, and in the **Archive** album
  here, which appears once something is archived.
- To bring photos back, select them (in the Archive album) and choose
  **Photo > Bring back from the archive** - the same command and key.
- **Move the Archive to a drive:** **Library > Move the Archive to a
  drive...** opens the [Migrate](Migration.md) page with every source ticked
  and *Only photos in the Archive* on. Choose the archive drive as the
  target: archived photos are copied there, checked, and only then set aside
  from their old place (into quarantine, as always). They stay archived and
  in the Archive album.

## Smart albums

A smart album is a set of rules that picks photos by itself - and keeps
picking them as your photos change. **Albums > New smart album...**, give it
a name, then add rules:

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Stars, ISO, Aperture, Focal length, Shutter (seconds) | ISO > 3200 |
| Camera, Lens | Lens contains 24-70 |
| Flag, Colour label, Kind (photo / RAW / video) | Flag is pick |
| Date taken | on or after 2025-01-01 |
| Tag | has Trips > Vegas |
| Search words | matches sunset beach |

Choose whether a photo must match **all** of the rules or **any** of them.
Right-click a smart album to edit its rules, rename or remove it (removing
it never touches a photo).

## Automatic

Lunelis keeps these up to date by itself:

| Album | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Favorites | Photos with 4 or 5 stars |
| Picks | Everything you picked |
| Videos | Every video |
| RAW files | Straight from the camera |
| Recently imported | The last 30 days (hidden while that's the whole library) |
| Screenshots | Files with "screenshot" in their name or folder |
| One per camera | Your eight most-used cameras, by name (e.g. α7R V, Galaxy S21 Ultra) |

Automatic albums with nothing in them aren't shown.
